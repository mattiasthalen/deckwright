# Brawl rats, the template

A rat player whose commander is chosen and who stops once the template is set:
the cheap way to see how categories are named when a creature type is a criterion.

## Opening

Let's carry on with my Brawl deck. Its files are in the deck folder here.

## Start

brawl-rats-criteria

## Player

You want a blue-black rat deck with Vren, the Relentless as commander. Any set is
fine and you have no card limits. The commander is chosen; nothing else is.

Answer questions plainly. When you are shown a template (the groups of cards the
deck will have), accept it as proposed. Once the assistant says the template is
saved, you have to go: stop.
