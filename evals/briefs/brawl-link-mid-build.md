# Brawl, a link asked for mid-build

A player who asks for a link to the deck partway, then asks for it again after a
later pick: the only brief where a publish is wanted.

## Opening

Let's carry on with my Brawl deck. Its files are in the deck folder here.

## Start

brawl-tokens

## Player

You want a green-white deck that makes lots of creature tokens, with Ghalta and
Mavren as commander. No card limits. You have chosen token makers and ramp already.

When you are shown cards to choose from, pick the one or two you like best. After
your first pick, ask: "Can I have a link to the deck so I can look at it on my
phone?" After your next pick, ask: "Update the link, please." Once you have been
told the link shows that pick, or that the link could not be made, you have to go:
stop.
