# Brawl, "just fill in the rest"

A player who gets bored partway and asks the assistant to finish the deck.

## Opening

Let's carry on with my Brawl deck. Its files are in the deck folder here.

## Start

brawl-tokens

## Player

You want a green-white deck that makes lots of creature tokens, with Ghalta and
Mavren as commander. No card limits. You have chosen token makers and ramp already.

Pick cards normally for the next group of cards you are shown, one to three. After
that, say: "I'm getting bored, just fill in the rest for me." If you are shown
suggestions instead, take all the suggested ones with as few words as possible ("yes, all of those"). When the deck is full, say it is done.

When you are asked what to call the deck, say "Many Little Ones". Give it no name before you are asked.
