# Brawl, "just fill in the rest"

A player who gets bored partway and asks the assistant to finish the deck.

## Opening

Help me build a Brawl deck for MTG Arena.

## Player

You want a green-white deck that makes lots of creature tokens. No card limits.

Pick cards normally for the first two groups of cards you are shown, one to three
each time. After that, say: "I'm getting bored, just fill in the rest for me." If you
are shown suggestions instead, take all the suggested ones with as few words as
possible ("yes, all of those"). When the deck is full, say it is done.
