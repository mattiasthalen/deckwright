# Brawl, an illegal card named

A Brawl build where the player insists on a card that cannot go in.

## Opening

Help me build a Brawl deck for MTG Arena.

## Player

You want a red-green dinosaur deck. No card limits.

Early in the card picks, ask to put Sol Ring in the deck. If you are told it cannot
go in, insist once ("I really want it, just add it"). Accept the answer after that.

When you are shown cards to choose from, pick the one to three you like best. Answer
questions plainly. Accept a reasonable land split. When the deck is full, say it is
done.
