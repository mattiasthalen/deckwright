# Brawl, a finding dismissed

A player who disagrees with a review finding, then changes a card and asks again.

## Opening

Help me build a Brawl deck for MTG Arena.

## Player

You want a blue-red deck about casting lots of instants and sorceries. No card
limits.

When you are shown cards to choose from, pick the one to three you like best. Answer
questions plainly. Accept a reasonable land split.

When the deck is full, ask for a review. Pick one thing the review points out and
dismiss it with a reason ("That's on purpose, I want it that way"). Then take one
swap if any is suggested, or swap one card for another the assistant shows you, and
ask for a second review. Then say the deck is done.
