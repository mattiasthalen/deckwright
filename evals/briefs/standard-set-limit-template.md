# Standard, a set limit, to the template

A Standard player who wants only cards from one set, and stops once the template
is set: the cheap way to see how cards "from a set" are counted.

## Opening

Help me build a Standard deck for MTG Arena.

## Player

You want a white-blue deck about spacecraft and artifacts. Your limit: only cards
that have a printing in Edge of Eternities. Nothing else is limited.

Answer questions plainly. When you are shown a template (the groups of cards the
deck will have), accept it as proposed. Once the assistant says the template is
saved, you have to go: stop.
