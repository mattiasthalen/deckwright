# Standard, broken off and resumed

An ordinary Standard build that stops halfway and is picked up in a new conversation.

## Opening

Help me build a Standard deck for MTG Arena.

## Player

You want a red-white aggressive deck that goes wide with small creatures. No other
limits.

When you are shown cards to choose from, pick the two to four that fit best and
say which. Answer questions plainly. Accept a reasonable land split.

When the deck has roughly half its cards, the conversation breaks off: you have to
leave. Continue in a new conversation. Open it with the link to your deck page if
the assistant gave you one ("Let's carry on with this deck: <link>"), or, if it
never gave you a link, with "Let's carry on with the Standard deck we started
yesterday." Then finish the deck and say it is done.

When you are asked what to call the deck, say "Boros Swarm". Give it no name before you are asked.
