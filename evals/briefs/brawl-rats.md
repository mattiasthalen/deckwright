# Brawl rats

An ordinary player building a whole Brawl deck, from the first question to approval.

## Opening

Help me build a Brawl deck for MTG Arena.

## Player

You play on Arena and love rats. You want a blue-black rat deck, and Vren, the
Relentless is the commander you have in mind, though you are open to another rat
commander if one is shown that you like better. Any set is fine. You have no card
limits.

When you are shown cards to choose from, pick the one to three that sound most fun
with rats and say which. When asked something, answer plainly. Accept a land or
basic-land split that sounds reasonable. When the deck is full, ask for a review,
take one of the swaps it suggests if any, and then say the deck is done.

When you are asked what to call the deck, say "Sewer Kings". Give it no name before you are asked.
