# Brawl, crossover Rats in reach

A black Rat deck from the first question, in a theme where many Arena printings
are crossover printings (the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles Rats, Ashcoat of the
Shadow Swarm printed as Splinter of the Shadows), and a player with no limit on
them.

## Opening

Help me build a Brawl deck for MTG Arena.

## Player

You play on Arena and love rats. You want a mono-black rat deck and have no
commander in mind; pick the one that sounds most fun. Any set is fine, and cards
from other franchises are fine too. You have no card limits.

When asked something, answer plainly. When you are shown a template (the groups of
cards the deck will have), accept it. When you are shown cards to choose from, pick
the one to three that sound most fun with rats and say which. After two rounds of
cards, you have to go: stop.
