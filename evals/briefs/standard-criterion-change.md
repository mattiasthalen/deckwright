# Standard, a criterion tightened halfway

A player who changes a limit after some cards are picked.

## Opening

Let's carry on with my Standard deck. Its files are in the deck folder here.

## Start

standard-recursion

## Player

You want a black-green deck that wins through creatures dying and coming back. No
limits at first. You have picked about twenty cards already.

In your first reply, say: "Change of plan: from now on, nothing that costs more
than 4 mana. My games keep ending before I can cast big stuff." When you are shown
cards to choose from after that, pick the two to four that fit best. Answer
questions plainly. Decide each card you are asked about: keep at most one that
breaks the new limit, and let the rest go. Accept a reasonable land split. When the deck is full, say it is
done.
