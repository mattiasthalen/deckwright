# Standard, a criterion tightened halfway

A player who changes a limit after some cards are picked.

## Opening

Help me build a Standard deck for MTG Arena.

## Player

You want a black-green deck that wins through creatures dying and coming back. No
limits at first.

When you are shown cards to choose from, pick the two to four that fit best. Answer
questions plainly.

Once you have picked about twenty cards, say: "Change of plan: from now on, nothing
that costs more than 4 mana. My games keep ending before I can cast big stuff."
Decide each card you are asked about: keep at most one that breaks the new limit,
and let the rest go. Accept a reasonable land split. When the deck is full, say it is
done.
