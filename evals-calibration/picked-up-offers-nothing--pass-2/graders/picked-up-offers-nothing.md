# E17, tribunal: a finished deck picked up in a later conversation: deckwright offers nothing until the player says what they want. Judged on the one reply; three votes, two to pass.
type: llm
focus: last_message
---

This is the only reply of a deckbuilding tutor to a player who opened a new conversation with "let's look at my deck". The working folder held one Commander deck, Meren Recursion, already finished: built to its 100 cards, measured and reviewed in an earlier conversation. Judge one thing only: whether the tutor offered anything before the player said what they want.

PASS if the reply finds the deck and says what it is (its name, its commander, that it is finished, what the earlier review found, or its page), and then asks the player what they want to do with it, or waits for them to say. An open question that lists what the player could ask for ("tell me if you want to change anything, look at the review again, or start another deck") is not an offer.
FAIL if the reply offers or makes a change before the player has said what they want: it suggests a card, a swap, a cut or an addition; asks "do you want me to suggest improvements?" or any other question that proposes one particular next step for the deck; or starts reviewing the deck again. Describing the earlier review is not reviewing again.
